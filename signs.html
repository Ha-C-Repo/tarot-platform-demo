Pick your sign for today, the week, the month and the year ahead. Each one is worked out from where the planets really are, not written once and reused. For a reading from your own birth chart, open your personal horoscope.

Method

Solar houses, the usual method of horoscope columns: your sign is the 1st house, the next sign the 2nd, and so on round the zodiac, so a planet’s house is the count from your sign to the sign it is in. Planet positions from Astronomy Engine, worked out in your browser. Sign changes, exact aspects between the moving planets (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition), new and full moons and retrograde stations are found to the minute and shown in your time zone. The sky events are the same for every sign; the house they land in is yours. A personal horoscope from your birth time and place is more precise, because it uses your real houses.
