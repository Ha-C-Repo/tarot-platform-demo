Content line

Positive work only

Protection, cleansing, luck, fortune, blessings, wellbeing, skincare and haircare, self-knowledge, moon and seasonal practice, divination. No curses, no hexes, no binding, no love spells, no destiny swapping, and nothing worked on a named person without their consent. Historical material describing baneful practice appears as history, clearly framed, never as instruction.
