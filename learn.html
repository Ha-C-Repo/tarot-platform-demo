Flashcards and a quick quiz on the names, the meanings upright and reversed, and the astrology behind each card. The cards you get wrong come back more often until they stick.

How it picks the next card

Every card keeps a score in this browser. A right answer adds to its run; a wrong one starts the run again and counts against it. The next card is chosen at random, weighted towards the ones you miss, and never the same card twice in a row. Three right in a row and a card counts as learned. Meanings are the short keywords used across this site; the astrology is the Golden Dawn system the 1909 deck was drawn in.
