Why the full date, and the time

The zodiac year does not begin on 1 January. The popular zodiac rolls over at Lunar New Year (late January to mid February). BaZi and Four Pillars roll over at Lichun, the start of spring, around 3–5 February, at an exact moment. Every January birthday belongs to the previous animal year, and where the two boundaries differ, both answers are shown rather than one picked silently. On Lichun day itself, and for the hour pillar, only the birth time can decide.

Method

New moons, the December solstice and the solar terms come from Astronomy Engine. Lunar New Year follows the calendar rules (month 11 holds the solstice; a leap month is the first with no principal term), in China time. Year pillar turns at Lichun, month pillar at each of the twelve jie terms, day pillar at local midnight, hour pillar every two hours from 23:00. From 23:00 to midnight the hour stem follows the next day (the common “late Rat hour” rule). Birth time is taken in local standard time: if the clocks were on daylight saving, that hour is removed first. No correction to true solar time.
