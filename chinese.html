Why the full date, not the year

The zodiac year does not begin on 1 January. The popular zodiac rolls over at Lunar New Year (late January to mid February). BaZi and Four Pillars roll over at Lichun, the start of spring, around 3–5 February. Every January birthday belongs to the previous animal year, and where the two boundaries differ, both answers are shown rather than one picked silently.
