The crystal
ball

Ask the ball a question and watch the mists part. Behind the glass, two tarot cards are shuffled and drawn for you, one at a time, and the ball answers in its own voice. You never see the cards; you see what they show.

Method, stated

For each question a fresh 78-card deck is shuffled with the same shuffle as the card pull (your browser’s cryptographic random generator, each card upright or reversed with even odds) and the top card taken; the 77 left are shuffled again and the top card taken. The cards stay hidden. The ball’s answer is built from them: what each card’s picture shows, the line written for that exact pair of cards, and the pair’s reading for love, for work and money, or in general, chosen from the words of your question. Questions that start with “Will”, “Should”, “Is” and similar get a lean: both cards upright leans yes, both reversed leans no, one of each is divided. For entertainment and reflection. The texts are sample text written for this demo.
