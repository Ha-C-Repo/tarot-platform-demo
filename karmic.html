Method, stated

Tropical zodiac and the house system last chosen on the Birth chart page (Placidus unless changed). Mean lunar node: the South Node is the familiar past and default, the North Node the direction of growth. Planets within 8° of either node are read with it. Saturn shows the lesson, Jupiter the gift already earned, the twelfth house what works out of sight, retrograde planets what is done inwardly or on a second pass. The Vedic part uses the Lahiri sidereal zodiac: Ketu (the South Node) by nakshatra, and the Atmakaraka, Jaimini’s soul indicator, the planet furthest through its sign. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
