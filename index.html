The free tools

Everything here runs on real maths

No sign-up to try them. Each one shows its method, because a calculator that will not tell you how it got the answer is not worth much.

Card pull

Shuffle a real 78-card deck and choose a spread: one card, yes or no, past-present-future, love, the full moon, the zodiac houses or the Celtic Cross. Upright or reversed, each with its traditional meaning. Nothing about you changes what comes up.

Moon tracker

Phase, sign, illumination, the month ahead, and the next new and full.

Birth chart

The full natal chart: wheel, every planet’s sign and house, Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign or Equal houses, aspects, dignities and the balance of elements.

Your horoscope

Personal, from your own chart: what the planets are doing to it today, the exact dates of each contact this month, the slow transits of the year and the retrograde calendar.

Compatibility

Two birth charts compared: rising signs, houses and real planetary aspects, with the weighting published. A dates-only quick version too.

Numerology

Life path, expression, soul urge, personal year, and your tarot birth cards. Pythagorean, method stated.

Chinese astrology

Animal, element and the Four Pillars, computed from the real lunisolar calendar and the exact solar terms — not a year lookup table.

Maya calendar

Tzolk’in, Haab’ and Long Count, on the traditional count.
