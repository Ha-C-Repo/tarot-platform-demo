How the deck works

The deck is a real, ordered deck of all 78 cards, each lying upright or reversed. Shuffle puts the cards still in the deck into a new random order and turns each one upright or reversed at random, using your browser’s cryptographic random number generator. Cards you have already pulled stay where they are in the spread, out of the deck, so you can shuffle between pulls. That order then stays exactly as it is until the next shuffle, even if you close the page and come back. Pull deals the top card. New reading puts the pulled cards back under the deck without shuffling, so if you choose not to shuffle, your next reading comes off the same deck in the same order.

Every order of the deck is equally likely, and nothing the site knows about you changes the odds or the wording.
