How the draw works

A uniform shuffle of all 78 cards using crypto.getRandomValues, dealt without replacement, with an independent 50/50 reversal per card. Nothing the site knows about you changes the odds or the wording. The draw is seeded by the date so you get the same three cards if you reload today.
