Recorded and live are different things

The monthly reading included with the subscription is recorded: 8 to 10 minutes, made for you, sent to you, yours to rewatch. It is not a call. A live session is a separate booking where you are in the room and can ask questions back. Both are real readings; they are not the same product, and the difference is stated here so nobody subscribes expecting the other one.

For the reader looking at this demo

Set your own reading price in Customise, bottom right, and this page updates. The arithmetic behind the middle tier: at 50 seats and a $85 rate that is $4,250 a month for roughly 21 hours of recording, against about $1,190 for the same hours spent on live sessions. The cap is what makes it deliverable — an uncapped tier sells more subscriptions than there are hours in the month.
