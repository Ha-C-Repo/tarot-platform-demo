Rules, published

Each day is judged at local noon. The Moon in one of the signs that suit the activity adds 2. A waxing Moon for things you start, or a waning one for things you end or rest from, adds 2 (the wrong phase takes 1). Mercury retrograde takes 3 for talk, travel and contracts; Venus retrograde takes 3 for love and money. A Moon void of course for half the waking hours (08:00 to 22:00) takes 2. With your birth chart, the Moon within 6° of a conjunction, sextile or trine to the planets that matter for the activity adds 2; a square or opposition takes 1. Traditional electional rules of thumb, the same for everyone without a chart, and never random.

Method

Positions from Astronomy Engine. Solar return: the moment the Sun returns to its exact birth longitude, cast for where you are. Progressions: secondary (one day after birth for each year of life); progressed angles by solar arc. Returns: each pass of Saturn or Jupiter over its birth position, and the Moon’s return to its birth position. Eclipses: Astronomy Engine’s eclipse search, at the moment of greatest eclipse; “touches” means within 3° of a point in your chart. Void-of-course Moon: from the Moon’s last exact major aspect to any planet, Sun to Pluto, until it enters the next sign. Planetary hours: daylight and night each split into twelve hours from the local sunrise and sunset, in the Chaldean order starting from the day’s ruler. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
