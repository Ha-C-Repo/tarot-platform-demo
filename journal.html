Your
reading journal

Every reading you finish on the card pull lands here by itself, with your question and the cards in their places. Add a note when something comes of it, and over time you can see which cards keep turning up.

Where your journal lives

Only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can read it. That also means clearing your browser data, or Reset demo data, wipes it. Back up to a file saves the whole journal as one small file you keep; Load a backup adds the readings from that file, here or in another browser, without touching the ones already here.

On a live site

With accounts switched on, the journal follows the visitor to every device they sign in on, and the reader can offer to look over a client’s journal before a private session, with the client’s permission.
