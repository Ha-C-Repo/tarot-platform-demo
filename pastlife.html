Past lives

A past-life reading from your birth chart, in the tradition of Edgar Cayce’s planetary sojourns, joined with the lunar nodes, Vedic astrology, numerology and the tarot. Where two systems agree, the report says so first.

Method, stated

Interlife sojourns: Edgar Cayce taught that souls spend time in planetary realms between earth lives. Following the past-life astrology built on his readings, the sidereal Sun and Moon are each placed in a decanate (a 10° third of a sign; the three decanates of a sign are that sign and the next two of its element), and the decanate’s ruling planet is read as the realm of the sojourn. Sidereal zodiac: Fagan-Bradley by default, Lahiri on request, both as Swiss Ephemeris defines them.

Settings and roles: the South Node’s sign (setting) and house (role), the Vedic Ketu, and the major aspects. Numbers: Pythagorean, with karmic debt numbers (13, 14, 16, 19 on the way to a core number) and karmic lessons (digits missing from the full birth name). Leave the name as “You” to skip the name numbers.

How the systems are combined: each system votes for planets. Numbers vote through the Chaldean planet of each number (1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Uranus, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Neptune, 8 Saturn, 9 Mars); the birth chart through its three strongest planets (angular, dignified, conjunct a light, well aspected); the sojourns, the Vedic soul indicator (Atmakaraka) and the ruler of the South Node vote too. A planet backed by two or more systems is a core theme. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
